About me

I'm a PhD candidate in Human–Robot Interaction at Singapore Management University, where I study how robots can work with people safely, intuitively and helpfully.

I finished my B.Sc. Engineering (Hons) in Mechanical Engineering, specializing in Mechatronics, at the University of Moratuwa in July 2026. During my degree I was a Research Intern at the National University of Singapore, where I worked on a guided robotic dog for visually impaired people. That project got me interested in robots built around the people who use them.

From 2024 to September 2026 I was part of Team Falcon E Racing. I started on the vehicle dynamics team, then became Vehicle Dynamics Engineer and Chief Operating Officer. I was also active in MoraSpirit, the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society and the Mora Hiking Club.

Outside research, I play badminton and cricket and build small robotics and automation projects.
